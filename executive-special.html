02 / A COMPARISON WE CAN QUESTION

A fairer test is
designed, not assumed.

A useful comparison needs common ground, independent checks and openness about what differs.

Read the bias review ↗
01

Same situations. Same checks.

Services face the same scenario catalogue, simulated bank and scoring rules. The latest assessment repeats each situation three times.

02

Differences are visible.

Provider-specific prompts and some delivery and timing differences remain. Results describe configured services, not isolated models.

03

We check the test itself.

An earlier review identified a latency measurement bias. It was corrected, and remaining timing differences are disclosed. We do not name a fastest service.

These controls reduce and expose bias; they do not prove every possible bias has been eliminated.

03 / THE EVIDENCE

What did we learn
from the tests?

Across 720 runs, six configured services faced 40 situations three times each. These are the findings that matter for a business decision.

01 / OVERALL QUALITYNo clear winner

Two services shared the lead.

Gemini Live and ElevenLabs Agents tied on the report’s quality score, which excludes latency. The assessment could not distinguish an overall quality winner.

02 / PROTECTION0 prohibited actions

Controls held across 720 runs.

No prohibited actions were executed with the services and harness controls working together. This result does not isolate provider behaviour from those controls.

03 / CUSTOMER OUTCOMES28–33 / 33

Completing the request varied.

Gemini, ElevenLabs and xAI completed 33 of 33 eligible runs each; Nova completed 28. One scenario was excluded because its script offered no clarification turn.

04 / SPOKEN FACTS14 / 18 to 21 / 21

Correct facts need their own check.

Gemini and ElevenLabs passed 21 of 21 applicable factual checks each. OpenAI Realtime passed 14 of 18; four were unresolved because the amount was ambiguous and need review.

05 / VOICE COST$0.0038 per run

Observed costs differed substantially.

Nova’s median voice cost was $0.0038, compared with $0.0193 for GPT-Realtime and $0.0333 for xAI. Two services could not be priced; some runs used for cost were separate from scored runs.

Speed needs context

Median first audio ranged from 1.33 to 1.92 seconds, but end-of-speech detection differed between services, so these figures do not establish a fair speed ranking.
