Conversations: common and tuned prompts

Blind listening study ↗

Six three-turn situations per provider, each recorded twice: once with a common prompt and once with a provider-specific prompt written from the vendor’s own prompting guidance. Every cell has both, so you can hear what tuning changes for the same provider and scenario. Recorded 29 October 2026 with a British female assistant voice where the vendor offers one (OpenAI and xAI have no British option) and a British male caller. The tuned prompts are documentation-informed starting prompts, not proven optimal tuning, and neither condition has tools or banking actions. Caller is on the left channel, assistant on the right. Provider identities are shown here; the blind study hides them. Prompt text and hashes are in the frozen plan.
