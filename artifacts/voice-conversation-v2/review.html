    What these recordings can tell you

    These are scripted three-turn exchanges with synthetic callers and simulated playback timing. Caller audio is on the left channel and assistant audio on the right; use both channels. They do not test live interruption handling or completed banking actions.

    Transcripts are provider-reported, not independently verified against the audio. Use them to assess wording; listen to assess voice clarity and delivery. The tuned prompts are starting configurations, not proven optimal. Two repeats per condition provide limited evidence of consistency.

    AI assessments, when shown, judge wording and behaviour from transcripts only. They are separate from your own ratings, and do not assess voice delivery. Model names reflect the recording metadata; ElevenLabs’ agent identifier does not identify its underlying language model.
