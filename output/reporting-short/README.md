# Reporting product short

24-second, 1920×1080 MP4 at 30 fps, with original electronic music and on-screen copy. No voiceover. Uses actual local HTML screenshots captured on 31 October 2026, animated with subtle motion and quick cuts.

Timeline:
- 0–3s: Big questions. Visible evidence.
- 3–6s: Executive briefing (`executive-trust.html`).
- 6–9s: Business dashboard comparison (`business-dashboard.html`).
- 9–12s: Run ledger and export controls.
- 12–15s: Instructions, configuration and assessors (`test-setup.html`).
- 15–18s: Listening library (`voice-library.html`).
- 18–21s: Research archive (`index.html`).
- 21–24s: Explore the reporting prototype.

Prototype label remains visible throughout. Screens show saved experimental evidence, not a live provider evaluation. The standard-prompt comparison includes historical GPT-Live carry-over, labelled in the source page; differing setup, timing and cost coverage must be considered before interpreting results. Listening recordings are separate studies. Connect/ACXD are not benchmarked. No provider calls were made and no application pages were changed.

Editable captions: `storyboard.json`; rendering and capture scripts: `.build/`. Music reused from the original locally generated product-spot soundtrack.
