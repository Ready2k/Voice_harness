OUR PURPOSE
Help teams ask better questions before deployment.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

A convincing demo
is only the beginning.

A voice service must do more than sound natural. In a banking conversation, a clear answer, a protected account and an appropriate response to uncertainty all matter.

01Verify identityDoes the service protect information before sharing it?
02Get the facts rightDo spoken amounts and details match the evidence?
03Respect consentDoes the service seek the confirmation required before acting?
04Handle difficultyWhat happens with interruptions, faults or a vulnerable caller?

02 / THE APPROACH

A test environment
built around the voice.

Voice Harness brings synthetic customers, a simulated bank, independent checks and retained evidence together. Teams can investigate the service and the controls around it.

See how it works ↗
01

Exercise realistic situations

Run a common catalogue of requests and conditions against configured services.

02

Check speech and actions

Assess proposed actions and spoken facts using independent controls and evidence.

03

Keep the evidence

Review what happened and verify retained evidence, rather than relying on a score alone.

03 / THE PRINCIPLES

Evidence with
its limits attached.

The harness is designed to make uncertainty visible and give reviewers a basis for further investigation.

WHAT THIS MEANS

A test result is a starting point.

Observed outcomes describe the configured services and harness controls working together in the situations tested. They are not a certification or proof of readiness for deployment.

Explore the executive briefing ↗
01

Evidence over assertion

Keep unknowns visible and follow results back to their supporting records.

02

Missing evidence is not a pass

When a check cannot be assessed, the result should say so.

03

People remain part of the decision

AI judge findings are advisory; reviewers must consider context, uncertainty and limitations.

04 / WHAT COMES NEXT

Build confidence
one question at a time.

The work continues: repeat tests where results vary, strengthen judge calibration and investigate capabilities that remain unknown.
