THE INTENT
Make voice behaviour inspectable, with its uncertainty intact.

01 / THE PROBLEM

Speech changes
what must be tested.

A speech-to-speech service can speak and call tools during the same conversation. Its audio may reach a person before anyone can read a transcript, so text-only evaluation cannot answer every question that matters.

01Identity & consentWas identity verified before disclosure or action? Was consent explicit and unambiguous?
02Spoken factsDid the caller hear amounts, dates and identifiers that match the system of record?
03Real call conditionsWhat happens with interruption, noisy audio, a failing tool or a vulnerable caller?
04Containment & proofIf a service misbehaves, was the harm stopped and can the outcome be verified later?

Providers differ in interruption, truncation, tool handling, usage reporting and transcripts. Those capabilities need evidence, not just vendor claims.

02 / THE APPROACH

Four jobs.
One reusable harness.

The aim is a common environment for examining different configured services, with independent controls and evidence throughout.

Explore the technical design ↗
01

Contain

Check tool proposals before execution and hold generated audio until policy allows release.

02

Exercise

Run scripted and model-driven scenarios across personas, locales, audio conditions, interruptions and injected faults.

03

Judge

Use deterministic safety checks and independent transcription; AI judges provide advisory findings subject to calibration.

04

Prove

Keep an append-only, hash-chained run ledger and export a signed bundle that can be verified offline.

03 / THE PRINCIPLES

Rules for evidence
we can trust and question.

These principles shape how the harness evaluates, reports and communicates uncertainty.

01

Evidence over assertion

Distinguish verified, observed, hypothesised, unsupported and unknown capabilities. Unknown stays unknown; capability reports expire.

02

Fail closed

Missing evidence is untestable, not a pass. Evidence-write failure blocks tool calls and audio release; empty independent transcription is not proof that no claim was made.

03

Coverage is not a ranking

Coverage describes support by provider family. Bake-offs may order results only with completeness and safety gates, uncertainty, rank stability and disclosed asymmetries.

04

Deterministic first

AI judges are advisory. No judge blocking policy is approved; a judge cannot change banking state or override a deterministic control.

05

Independent assessment

The judge is independent of the model under test. Assessment transcription is separate from the provider’s own transcript.

06

Honest limits

Silence fixtures test enforcement mechanics, not recognition accuracy. Single samples and repeated sets are labelled accordingly.

No certification claim.

A pass does not establish that a model is compliant, safe or fit for deployment. The harness is an evaluation environment, not a production banking service.

04 / WHO IT IS FOR

Built for teams
asking different questions.

The same retained evidence supports comparison, assurance and repeatable engineering work.

01

Engineering teams

Compare observed behaviour and verified capabilities when choosing or integrating a provider. Keep unestablished cost or latency values unknown.

02

Risk and assurance reviewers

Inspect findings, review judge disagreements and verify signed evidence offline.

03

Platform and tooling teams

Exercise the deterministic core in CI with a mock provider and no provider credentials.

05 / THE ROADMAP

Progress through
acceptance, not dates.

The documented roadmap is organised around acceptance criteria. These statuses reflect the source vision; consult the project status documents for the latest detail.

PHASE 0–3 / COMPLETE IN THE VISION

Establish the foundation

Verification and design, the deterministic core, initial adapters and coverage, followed by judging, signing, cost, observability and review.

PHASE 4 / IN PROGRESS

Broaden and deepen

More provider configurations, scenarios, faults, live runs, manual sessions, bake-offs and a telephone pilot.

PHASE 5 / ONGOING

Harden the system

Security review, load and chaos testing, reproducibility and regression gates.
